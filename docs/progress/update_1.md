## Project Discussion and Current Direction

We discussed several potential project ideas within the group, including:

* Fake News Detection
* Agricultural Disease Detection
* Mental Health Chatbot
* AI Medical Assistant
* Facial Recognition System

After evaluating these ideas, we have currently agreed to pursue a project focused on **3D Visualization of Core Computing Concepts**. The proposed system will aim to provide interactive and visually intuitive explanations of concepts that can be difficult for new students to understand through traditional theoretical material alone.

The initial focus is on **Data Structures and Algorithms (DSA)**, with the possibility of expanding the system to other core computing concepts, particularly those that can benefit from visual representation, such as Operating Systems concepts. We are also considering integrating an **AI-powered chatbot** that can provide additional explanations, answer questions, and assist students in understanding the concepts being demonstrated.

The exact scope and set of concepts to be included have not yet been finalized. We are currently evaluating the feasibility, educational value, and technical requirements of different concepts before defining the final project scope.

### Initial Research and Identified Gap

As part of our initial research, we reviewed several existing algorithm and computer science visualization projects available online. Based on our preliminary investigation, we identified a potential gap in existing visualization systems: **limited visualization of parallelism and concurrency concepts**.

Most existing visualization tools primarily focus on sequential algorithms and traditional data structures, while concepts involving **multithreading, concurrency, synchronization, and parallel algorithms** are comparatively less represented in interactive visualizations.

We therefore see an opportunity to incorporate these concepts into our proposed system. In addition to visualizing conventional DSA algorithms, the project could potentially demonstrate concepts such as **multithreading, race conditions, synchronization, parallel algorithms, and other concurrency-related problems** in an interactive 3D environment.

Further research and development are required to determine the final scope and the specific concepts that will be implemented.
