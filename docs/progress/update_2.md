## Supervisor Meeting and Guidance

We met with our FYP supervisor, **Mr. Mudassir Zaidi**, and presented our proposed project idea along with the other ideas we had previously considered. After discussing the different possibilities, our supervisor also showed greater interest in the **3D visualization of core computing concepts** project.

During the meeting, he discussed several potential implementation techniques and approaches that could be explored to develop the proposed system. He also provided guidance regarding the expected level of effort and contribution from each group member, as well as the criteria and general approach that will be used to evaluate our progress and project development.

Our supervisor instructed us to use the time leading up to our second meeting to conduct further research and gather more relevant material about the selected domain. We were also asked to develop a clearer understanding of the project's technical requirements and to ensure that the group is adequately prepared to proceed with its implementation.

Additionally, he asked us to assess and communicate our confidence in the **feasibility and successful implementation** of the proposed project. We will use the period before the next meeting to further investigate the technical challenges, refine the project scope, and evaluate whether the proposed features can be realistically implemented within the available time and resources.
