# Second Supervisor Meeting

During our second meeting with our supervisor, we discussed the current direction of our project, particularly our decision to use **Godot Engine with C#** as the primary development environment.

Our supervisor initially raised a concern that the proposed project might not involve enough actual programming or coding and could become primarily a visual or graphical project. We addressed this concern by explaining the programming components that will be involved in the project, including implementing the underlying simulations, algorithms, data structures, system behavior, user interaction, and logic required to visualize computing concepts. We also demonstrated how Godot and C# will be used not only for the visual presentation but also for the actual implementation of these concepts.

We also presented several possible areas of Computer Science that could be visualized through the application. Some of the concepts discussed included:

- **Memory Management**
- **Paging and Virtual Memory**
- **Process Scheduling**
- **CPU Scheduling**
- **Multithreading and Concurrency**
- **Deadlock and Resource Allocation**
- **Data Structures and Algorithms**
- **Time and Space Complexity**
- **Cache and Memory Hierarchy**

The meeting helped clarify that the project will combine **programming, simulation, and interactive visualization**, rather than functioning as a purely graphical application. We will continue evaluating these concepts and select the most suitable ones based on their educational value, implementation feasibility, and how effectively they can be represented through an interactive 2D/3D environment.

This meeting also helped us refine the overall direction of the project and gave us a clearer understanding of the technical depth expected from the final implementation.
